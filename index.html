How this works
  • Drag an empty spot to move around. Pinch, scroll, or use + and − to zoom.
  • Drag a name onto another seat, a table, or a + between chairs. On a phone, press and hold the name first.
  • Or tap a name, then tap the table where they should sit.
  • Tap a table to add or remove seats, move it or change its shape. Tap a green open seat to add someone.
  • Open the Planner at the top for guest counts, meals, VIPs, seating rules and the seat organizer.
  • Made a mistake? Press Undo at the top.
